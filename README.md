# Ferrari N.V. (NYSE/EXM: RACE) — Financial Modeling & DuPont Analysis (2020–2025)

An institutional-grade 6-year standardized financial model, comprehensive ratio engine, 3-Step & 5-Step DuPont pyramids, and multi-variable trend analysis dashboards for **Ferrari N.V.** constructed under **IFRS** and **Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)** financial analysis standards.

---

## 🏎️ Executive Summary & Key Highlights
* **Standardized 3-Statement Model:** Integrated Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement across 2020–2025 with dynamic balance checks.
* **D&A Unbundling & True Cash Margins:** Extracted non-cash industrial depreciation and amortization from Cost of Sales and R&D notes, isolating clean operating cash margins and matching reported EBITDA.
* **100% Official 2025 Tie-Out:** Exact mathematical reconciliation with Ferrari's official FY 2025 Investor Presentation:
  * **Net Revenues:** €7.15B (+7.0% YoY growth).
  * **EBITDA:** €2.77B at an exact **38.8% margin**.
  * **De-leveraging:** Net Debt to EBITDA reduced to **0.5x**.
* **Capital Structure & Debt Disaggregation:** Separated short-term borrowings from long-term bonds (Notes 24 & 27), reconciling Invested Capital against Net Operating Assets.
* **Working Capital Dynamics:** Quantified Ferrari’s unique luxury cash cycle, tracking the transition from historical negative Cash Conversion Cycles (-43.5 days in 2020) to lean positive cycles (+50 days in 2025) amid hybrid launches.
* **DuPont Decomposition:** Deconstructed ROE (>40%) to demonstrate that high equity returns stem from operating pricing power (EBIT margin expansion towards ~30%) rather than balance sheet leverage.

4. **`3-Step DuPont Pyramid`**: ROE decomposition into Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Financial Leverage.
5. **`5-Step DuPont Pyramid`**: Advanced operational breakdown isolating Tax Burden, Interest Burden, and Operating Margins.
6. **`Trend Analysis Dashboards`**: Dynamic multi-variable trend charts with statistical baselines across **Profitability**, **Utilization**, **Leverage**, and **Growth**.
